========================================
GMAIL SETUP - SIMPLE 3-STEP PROCESS
========================================

STEP 1: Enable 2-Step Verification
-----------------------------------
1. Open: https://myaccount.google.com/security
2. Find "2-Step Verification" 
3. Click "GET STARTED" (if it's OFF)
4. Follow the prompts (you'll need your phone)
5. Turn it ON

✓ Done? Move to Step 2


STEP 2: Generate App Password
-----------------------------------
1. Open: https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
2. Select app: "Mail"
3. Select device: "Windows Computer"
4. Click "GENERATE"
5. COPY the 16-character password (you won't see it again!)
6. Remove all spaces from the password

Example: 
  Google shows: abcd efgh ijkl mnop
  You use:      abcdefghijklmnop

✓ Done? Move to Step 3


STEP 3: Give Me Your Details
-----------------------------------
Tell me:
1. Your Gmail address: ____________________
2. Your App Password (16 chars, no spaces): ____________________

I'll update your .env file immediately!


========================================
HAVING TROUBLE?
========================================

Problem: "App passwords not available"
Solution: Make sure 2-Step Verification is ON, wait 5 minutes, try again

Problem: "Can't find 2-Step Verification"
Solution: Go to https://myaccount.google.com/security and look under "How you sign in to Google"

Problem: "Lost my App Password"
Solution: Go back to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords, delete old one, create new one
